# Recruiting lead-list rules — for confirmation

Superseded for the reviewer handoff by `imo-company-rules-for-review.md`.
The broader questions below are retained as history, not the current request.

Purpose: Find insurance producers we can recruit for Life, final expense, IUL,
and fixed annuities. These rules use the approved examples and public license
information. An omitted person is not automatically rejected.

## 1. Check whether the person is a suitable prospect

- Check the qualification called **Life**. It must say **Active**.
- Do not rely only on the overall license status.
- Do not approve for this pool if the displayed qualifications clearly show no
  active general Life authority.
- If qualifications are missing or unclear, mark **CHECK**, not **REJECT**.
- Reject a current **State Farm or Farmers relationship** under our confirmed
  company rule. Do not automatically reject Foremost alone.
- Keep WFG and Primerica producers in the accepted audience.
- Other Life producers and people with no displayed agency/appointments can
  also qualify. No displayed affiliation does not prove independence.
- A P&C license, variable authority, or a new license does not by itself reject
  the person. Approved examples have these features.
- Do not require proof that the person already sells final expense, IUL, or
  fixed annuities. The collected license pages do not show actual product sales.

## 2. Check whether we can contact the person

- **READY TO CONTACT:** suitable prospect with usable business contact details.
- **SAVE — NEEDS CONTACT:** suitable prospect without usable contact details.
  Save the record for later contact research. Do not reject the person.
- Check shared-office and toll-free numbers for reachability.
- Record where each phone/email came from. A displayed number is not proof
  that it is current or reaches the person directly.

Additional questions:

1. **Health or auto/home focus:** If Life is Active but the other relationships
   mainly show health or auto/home insurance, do we accept, give lower priority,

   health and P&C relationships.
2. **Licensing restrictions:** For an active Life license with a restriction
   order, should we check the current terms before approval? Do not treat an old
   restriction that was removed as a current restriction.
3. **Territory:** California is the default. Should an otherwise suitable
   nonresident lead be saved for another territory or excluded from this list?
4. **Any other hard rule:** Are there additional company exclusions or definite

## 4. Keep four final outcomes

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **READY TO CONTACT** | Meets confirmed fit rules and has usable contact details |
| **SAVE — NEEDS CONTACT** | Meets confirmed fit rules; contact research is needed |
| **CHECK** | A company rule, territory, or licensing fact is unresolved |
| **REJECT** | Fails a confirmed rule; record the rule and the source fact |

Known from the approved group: all 136 had active Life; 25 had no displayed
agency/insurer affiliation; 6 had active P&C authority; 4 had variable authority;
newer and experienced license holders were both approved. These examples rule
out blanket exclusions based on those traits.

Keep original approval separately from this screening result. Possible missed
approvals remain review candidates, not proof of a new exclusion.
