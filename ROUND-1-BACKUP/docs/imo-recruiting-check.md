## Meaning of the result

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | Fits the producer-fit screen. Contact readiness is recorded separately; not a new approval by the father. |
| **FAIL** | Meets a confirmed exclusion or lacks active general Life authority for this recruiting pool. |
| **REVIEW** | An unresolved geography, company/channel rule, or licensing issue prevents a confident fit decision. Give the exact issue and what would resolve it. |

Separately retain **father-approved / not in the father's supplied list /
not previously evaluated**. Never substitute a predicted PASS for his approval.
An omitted person who passes the screen is an unexplained omission, not proof
that the original list was wrong.

The user clarified that the approver did not necessarily obtain all records.
Use approved examples as positive evidence; omissions are weaker evidence of
possible filters, not confirmed rejections. Assume most may have been reviewed,
but retain the possibility of missed records.

### Contact readiness is separate from producer fit

- **READY TO CONTACT:** an otherwise acceptable producer has a usable business
  direct individual business contact for the intended outreach method.
- **SAVE — NEEDS CONTACT:** otherwise acceptable, but no usable contact is
  collected. Preserve the lead for later enrichment; do not disapprove it.
- Missing contacts can explain absence from the original simplified outreach
  dataset. Enrichment costs money and should be prioritized separately.
- Shared-office and toll-free numbers are not acceptable preferred contacts
  for the ready-to-contact list. If those are the only numbers, save the
  otherwise suitable producer for direct-contact research. They may have been
  accidentally included in the original approvals; do not infer acceptance
  from those examples. Keep original contact sources for provenance.

### Product sales are not observable screening requirements

The collected license pages show qualifications and line-specific relationships,
not proof of actual final-expense, IUL, or fixed-annuity sales. Do not require
proof of current target-product production in a public-record lead screen.
Carrier/product-focus markers can inform prioritization or a reviewer-confirmed
rule; they must not silently become evidence of sales or automatic exclusions.

- **REVIEW** if the page cannot be retrieved, identity does not match, or the
  relevant qualification cannot be verified. Missing data is not a FAIL.

### 4. PASS when no unresolved screen issue remains

verified bank/securities agency relationship, and no unresolved concrete
identity, relationship-status or licensing issue. Contact readiness and
priority are separate. Health/P&C-only displayed relationships remain eligible
at lower priority. Do not require proof of current target-product sales.

Positive evidence includes:

- **Direct Life appointments and a Life-oriented carrier mix:** supports

## Useful information that changes outreach, not pass/fail by itself

- **Life issue date:** years since the displayed issue date helps choose a
  training/onboarding versus established-broker growth conversation. It is not
  the person's age, continuous experience, or production history. No minimum
  or maximum tenure cutoff is confirmed.
- **A&H authority:** useful optional cross-selling capability; not required.
- **Variable Life/annuity authority:** useful context about a financial-advice
  practice; neither required nor a hard negative for this fixed-product focus.
- **Appointment effective dates:** recent contracting may guide conversation;
  it does not prove churn, production, or a willingness to change agencies.
- **Expiration:** flag upcoming renewal for onboarding. Do not fail an active
  license solely because renewal is near.
- **Carrier count:** shows relationship breadth, not sales volume. Many carriers
  at a bank do not automatically mean independent-broker fit.
- **Reported languages:** retain for outreach planning; apply no preference
  without a stated business need. Never infer language or ethnicity from names.
- **Sponsors:** retain the qualification-specific relationship. Do not assume
  that a P&C sponsor controls the person's Life business.
- **Complaints/orders:** retain exact public facts and dates; distinguish
  resolved history from current issues. Do not fabricate a universal exclusion.
