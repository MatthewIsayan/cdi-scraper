# Recruiting-screen instructions

Before searching, scraping, evaluating, or ranking insurance producers for this
project, read `docs/imo-recruiting-check.md` and follow its screening rules.
The finalized operating checklist is `docs/imo-final-approval-rules.md`;
it supersedes earlier proposed review questions and routing.

The user's IMO recruits for Life, final expense, IUL, and fixed annuities.
Preserve the distinction between a screening result and the father's actual
approval. Confirmed company exclusions include State Farm, Farmers, Allstate,
Knights of Columbus, Thrivent, and all verified current bank/securities agency
relationships. New York Life/NYLIFE and Northwestern Mutual
are acceptable. Other company/channel patterns must not silently become hard
exclusions; preserve the explicit working assumptions recorded in the rulebook.
Seek Life agencies; auto/home agency involvement is not a positive target
signal. Require direct contact for outreach readiness; save otherwise suitable
shared-office/toll-free-only or missing-contact leads for contact research.

Keep the rulebook updated when the user explicitly confirms a new rule or an
exception. Record the change and its basis. Do not invent rejection reasons to
make a model match the original list.

The original approval reference is `exports/aguilar-review/approved.json`.
Cached evidence and the prior comparison are under `exports/aguilar-review/`.
Refresh public records for new screening work and record the check date.
