# Company rules for our recruiting list

Superseded by `imo-approval-understanding.md`: the user requested positive
examples and stronger working assumptions rather than a neutral questionnaire.

We want to copy your selection rules for future lead searches. Normal licensing
checks are handled separately. Missing contacts mean save for research, not
reject. Omitted names may have been missed; the counts are not rejection labels.

## Already supported

- Exclude State Farm and Farmers under the confirmed rule.
- Do not automatically exclude WFG or Primerica.
- Do not automatically exclude AAA, Guardian, or Foremost: approved examples
  have these relationships. Do not extend this to similarly named companies.
- Auto/home qualifications, variable authority, no appointments, and license
  tenure do not cause rejection by themselves; approved examples vary on them.
- Actual IUL, final-expense, or annuity sales are not shown on the collected
  licensing pages and are not a requirement for this public-record screen.

## 2. What exact entry triggers each company exclusion?

- **Agency:** the named company appears in the agency section.
- **Appointment:** an appointment with the named insurer is enough.
- **Qualification:** the appointment must be for Life or another specified line.
- **Combination:** specify which company/agency entries must appear together.

Example: excluding **NYLIFE Securities agency relationships** differs from
excluding **every New York Life insurer appointment**. The observed group
includes New York Life Insurance and Annuity Corporation, New York Life
Insurance Company, and NYLIFE Insurance Company of Arizona. Do not silently
combine those entities into one rule.

## 3. Does an excluded relationship override acceptable relationships?

If someone has an excluded relationship AND companies we normally accept,
do we still reject them?

- **Yes:** the excluded relationship always wins.
- **No:** specify the evidence or exception that makes the person acceptable.

## Final rule format

**Exact company name + where it appears + qualification if relevant + exception.**

Do not turn unconfirmed company patterns into automatic rejections. This
handoff does not need geography, sales activity, discipline, or generic
"anything else?" questions.
