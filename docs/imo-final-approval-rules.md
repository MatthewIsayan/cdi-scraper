# IMO recruiting approval rules

Final operating rules - October 8, 2026

Target: California public-license leads for Life, final expense, IUL and fixed
annuities. Use these rules for any surname. This is a recruiting screen, not
proof of policy production or a new personal approval by the father.

## 1. Excluded companies and agencies

If a relevant current agency or insurer relationship matches an exclusion,
do not add the producer. An acceptable relationship elsewhere does not cancel it.

- State Farm
- Farmers
- Allstate
- Knights of Columbus
- Thrivent
- All verified bank or securities agencies, including newly encountered ones.
  Examples: Chase Insurance Agency; Wells Fargo Wealth Brokerage Insurance
  Agency; Edward Jones Insurance Agency of California; Merrill Lynch Life
  Agency; LPL Financial. This list of examples is not exhaustive.

Match actual current entries. State Farm/Farmers include their named insurance
entities. Ignore terminated relationships and incidental mentions. Do not expand
a named-company exclusion to differently named affiliates solely by ownership.
Foremost alone is not a Farmers exclusion. Ordinary Life insurers with "Bank"
in their name are not bank agencies. Variable authority alone is not a securities
agency relationship. Verify an unfamiliar agency's business; uncertainty means
review, not a guessed exclusion.

## 2. Eligible profiles

Eligible means the feature itself does not disqualify the producer. Exclusions
and licensing checks still apply.

- WFG and Primerica.
- New York Life / NYLIFE and Northwestern Mutual.
- Other Life insurers and agencies; no required carrier or carrier count.
- AssuredPartners, Acrisure, Filice, IMA and other large/multi-line brokers.
- Gallagher Benefit Services, Mercer Health, Marsh & McLennan and HUB remain
  eligible. Do not exclude a whole benefits-broker or corporate family.
- Life plus health, Property/Casualty, Personal Lines or variable qualifications.
- Only auto/home or health relationships displayed: eligible, lower priority.
  Approved counterexamples exist; do not apply a blanket rejection.
- No agencies or insurer appointments displayed: eligible, lower priority.
  25 original approvals had neither displayed. Absence does not prove lack of
  sales, independence or a need for beginner training.
- New or experienced license holders. No minimum tenure or production threshold
  is established by these records.
- Missing direct contact: retain the suitable lead for contact research.

The last three boundary decisions were finalized after the user accepted the
assistant's proposed answers and requested a final rule document. Eligibility
does not establish the historical approver's reasoning for omitted people.

## 3. Priority

- **Higher priority:** visible Life agency relationships or Life appointments
  aligned with the recruiting focus. WFG/Primerica are established audiences.
- **Lower priority:** only auto/home or health relationships displayed, or no
  agency/insurer relationships displayed. Keep eligible; process after stronger
  Life-business signals. Auto/home agency membership alone is not a target signal.
- Appointments show relationships, not proof of actual final-expense, IUL or
  annuity sales. Do not require sales proof from a licensing page.

## 4. Contact readiness

- **READY TO CONTACT:** eligible producer with a usable direct individual
  business contact for the outreach method. For phone outreach, use a number
  that reaches the producer.
- **SAVE - NEEDS CONTACT:** otherwise eligible, but contact is missing or only
  shared-office/toll-free numbers are available. Find direct contact before
  outreach; do not reject producer fit because of this.

Priority and contact readiness are separate fields. A high-priority lead may
still need contact research.

## 5. Short checklist for the next agent

1. Open a fresh CDI record and match identity by license number. Record URL and
   check date; deduplicate by license number.
2. Verify active general Life authority internally. Verified inactive/no Life
   is FAIL; an unavailable/missing qualification record is REVIEW. Limited
   burial authority alone does not qualify for this general-Life pool.
3. Check all current relevant agency/insurer entries against exclusions.
   A match is FAIL, even alongside acceptable relationships.
4. If no exclusion applies, do not reject eligible features in section 2.
   Health/P&C-only and no-relationship profiles receive lower priority.
5. Record fit as PASS / FAIL / REVIEW, plus priority and contact readiness.
   REVIEW requires a concrete identity, licensing, relationship-status or
   agency-classification uncertainty. Do not invent extra business-policy holds.
6. Retain exact source entries and rule triggers. Keep the father's historical
   approval status separate. An omitted name is not itself a rejection reason.

No new company exclusion should be invented from a small zero-approved group.
Low-volume ambiguous categories need not generate repeated policy questions;
keep factual notes and handle actual record uncertainty as REVIEW. Confirm new
general exclusions with the user and record changes.

## Basis and limits

Rules reflect user-confirmed exclusions and the finalized operating choices.
Historical evidence: 136 supplied approvals and cached CDI records checked
October 8, 2026. Not a fresh scrape of every producer or a measured accuracy test.
